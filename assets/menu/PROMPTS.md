Asset type: wide 16:9 background illustration for a videogame main menu, 1920x1080 composition.
Primary request: MIDNIGHT SHIFT, a surreal after-hours supermarket trolley racing game. A striking art-directed mixed-media silkscreen illustration with cut-paper angular silhouettes, expressive distorted perspective and slight handmade halftone ink grain. Graphic energy of a rebellious Japanese game menu meets a surreal psychological platformer; original artwork, no existing characters.
Scene: a deserted supermarket at midnight with impossibly curved shelving and huge fluorescent light strips spiralling toward a luminous acid-yellow circular midnight clock high on the right. A large wire shopping trolley hurtles diagonally toward the viewer in the lower right, fisheye perspective, exaggerated rubber wheels, violet and teal steel, pink handle. Groceries float in its wake: a milk carton, striped soda can, small cereal box. Checkerboard floor bends like a ribbon. Moonlight and store fluorescents. The world feels mischievous, uncanny and kinetic.

Trolley centered at 77% x, 65% y, fully visible inside frame. No elements needed at very bottom 8%. Oversized clock at 82% x 22% y. Asymmetrical diagonal collage.
Palette: dark inky violet-black, desaturated teal and lavender shadows, electric acid yellow/chartreuse highlights, restrained hot coral pink. Cream paper highlights. Bold flat color blocks with beautiful shadow, printmaking texture and a few painterly details. Professional game key art, not 3D stock rendering, not cute mobile art, no photographic look.
Text: NONE. No title, no UI, no buttons, no labels, no lettering, no watermark. Make only the illustration, as a reusable background asset.
