**SHIFT MART — Night shift. Maximum chaos.**

Clock out and race supermarket trolleys through the aisles with friends.
This early Windows prototype supports direct-IP host/join for 3–5 riders,
three-lap races, ramming, repair and rematch. Solo debug mode is also available.

**Controls:** WASD/arrows to drive, hold R to recover, Esc for local menu.
**Online requirement:** host must be reachable on the selected UDP port. CGNAT can
prevent direct-IP access. No matchmaking or automatic NAT traversal is included.
**Development status:** route shifting, items, audio and final art are still pending.
Cross-home play and low-end performance have not yet been verified.
